If the app showed an error: Settings → Report a problem → Copy details, then paste it into the email. It holds error codes only, nothing from your vault.

Never send your vault password, your Recovery Key or anything from your vault. We will never ask for them.

Sending a screenshot

  1. 1Open your vault.
  2. 2In Settings, turn off “Block screenshots” (it asks for Face ID, fingerprint or the phone's passcode).
  3. 3Take the screenshot.
  4. 4Check it and crop out anything private.
  5. 5Turn “Block screenshots” back on.
