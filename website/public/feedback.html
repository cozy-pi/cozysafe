Taking a screenshot

Cozy Safe blocks screenshots by default. To take one, open your vault, go to Settings → Security and turn off “Block screenshots” (it asks for Face ID, fingerprint or the phone's passcode).

Turn it back on when you are done, and check the screenshot shows nothing private.

What helps us

  • Your app version (Settings → About) and your device.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot without anything private in it.

Never send your password, your Secret Key or anything from inside your vault: we never need them.
