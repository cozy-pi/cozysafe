Taking a screenshot

  1. 1Open your vault.
  2. 2Go to Settings → Display.
  3. 3Turn off “Block screenshots” (it asks for Face ID, fingerprint or the phone's passcode).
  4. 4Take your screenshot.
  5. 5Turn “Block screenshots” back on.
