Your notes, files and photos, locked on your own device.

Cozy Safe encrypts everything before it is saved or synced. Only your password opens it. Not us, not the cloud.

Download

Free and open source. macOS, Windows, Linux, Android; iPhone soon.

Early preview. You can create vaults, unlock and lock them. Notes, files, photos and sync come in the next releases. Keep copies of anything important elsewhere for now.

How it protects you

Encrypted on your device

Argon2id and XChaCha20-Poly1305 run before anything touches the disk or a cloud folder. Sync only ever moves ciphertext.

Password plus Secret Key

A random 128-bit Secret Key is mixed with your password, so a stolen copy of your vault cannot be guessed offline.

Open for anyone to check

Every line is public under GPL-3.0. Builds come from GitHub Actions with provenance you can verify.

What it does not do

No recovery

  • Forget your password and the vault is gone. Nobody can reset it.
  • Keep the Emergency Kit somewhere safe.

Honest limits

  • It cannot protect a device that already runs malware.
  • It does not hide that the app is installed.
  • Anyone who knows your password can open the vault.
