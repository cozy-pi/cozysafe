A private vault for your notes, documents and photos.

Everything is encrypted on your device before it is saved or synced. Without your password, nobody can open it, including us.

Download

Free and open source. For macOS, Windows, Linux and Android; iPhone is coming soon.

This is an early preview. You can create, unlock and lock vaults. Notes, files, photos and sync arrive in later releases, so don't keep the only copy of anything important here yet.

How Cozy Safe keeps you safe

Encrypted on your device

Data is encrypted before it reaches the disk or a cloud folder. Sync only ever sends encrypted data.

Password plus Secret Key

Your password is combined with a random 128-bit key kept on your device, so someone holding a copy of your vault still cannot guess the password.

Open source

Anyone can read and check the code (GPL-3.0). Installers are built by GitHub, with provenance you can verify.

Good to know first

There is no recovery

  • If you forget the password, nobody can open the vault again, including us.
  • Choose a password you will remember, such as a phrase of several words.

What it cannot do

  • It cannot protect a device that already has malware.
  • Others can still see that Cozy Safe is installed.
  • Anyone who knows the password can open the vault.
