On your phone and tablet

How Cozy Safe keeps you safe

Your data never leaves your device unencrypted, and the code that does it is public.

Encrypted on your device

Data is encrypted before it is written to the disk, so a backup kept in a cloud folder is encrypted too.

Vault password plus Recovery Key

Your vault password is combined with a random 256-bit Recovery Key (24 words), so someone holding a copy of your backup still cannot guess the password.

No server, no account

Cozy Safe talks to no server. The only connection it makes is to your own device on the same Wi-Fi, when you start a sync and allow it, and everything sent is encrypted.

Private on screen

Passwords and the Recovery Key stay hidden until you tap Show, copied text is wiped after 60 seconds, and the vault locks as soon as you leave the app.

Backups you control

Back up every vault to one encrypted file and open it on a new device with your Recovery Key and vault password. Nothing goes to our servers; we have none.

Open source

Anyone can read and check the code (GPL-3.0). Installers are built by GitHub, with provenance you can verify.

Good to know first

There is no recovery

  • If you lose your Recovery Key or your vault password, nobody can open your backup, including us.
  • Choose a password you will remember, such as a phrase of several words.

What it cannot do

  • There is no sync between devices yet: back up to a file often.
  • It cannot protect a device that already has malware.
  • Anyone who knows the password can open the vault.
